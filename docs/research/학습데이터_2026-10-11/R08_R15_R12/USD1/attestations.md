USD1 Attestations

_STABLECOIN ATTESTATIONS_

USD1 is powered by BitGo, the digital asset infrastructure company, safeguarding billions in digital assets for the largest institutions with multi-location and multi-jurisdictional security.

Continuing in this commitment to trust, BitGo publishes monthly reserve attestations to ensure transparency into the backing of USD1.

USD1 reserve holdings are disclosed by management on a monthly basis. An independent accounting firm examines management's assertion regarding the presentation and disclosure of USD1 redeemable tokens outstanding, the related redemption assets, and their comparison, in accordance with the 2025 AICPA Criteria for Asset-Backed Fiat-Pegged Tokens. Their reports are prepared in accordance with the attestation standards of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA).
