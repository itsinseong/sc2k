10.5 FX Operations
10.5.1. As part of its ancillary services5 under its Electronic Money Institution (EMI) license, Circle France offers fiat foreign exchange services (“FX Operations”) exclusively involving EUR and USD.
10.5.2. FX Operations do not involve the direct conversion of fiat currencies held by the client. Instead, the FX Operation is structured as a three-step process involving:
the redemption of e-money tokens (EURC or USDC) held by the client into fiat currency (EUR or USD, respectively);
a foreign exchange transaction performed by Circle France in the client’s name and on the client’s behalf, whereby the redeemed fiat currency is exchanged for its countervalue in the other currency (EUR into USD or USD into EUR); and
the immediate issuance of e-money tokens (USDC or EURC, respectively) against the fiat currency resulting from the FX transaction.
Each FX Operation is executed based on a single instruction from the client confirming its willingness to perform this full sequence.
10.5.3. The FX Operation is initiated by the client via the “Convert” functionality available in the Circle Mint Account interface. Before confirming the operation, the client is provided with the indicative exchange rate (EUR/USD or USD/EUR, as the case may be) applicable to the transaction and any applicable fees. By validating the operation, the client expressly authorises Circle France to proceed with the full FX Operation, and acknowledges that the operation involves three separate and consecutive steps which are executed immediately and without possibility of interruption. The client further agrees that the result of the FX Operation is the receipt of the corresponding amount of EURC or USDC, depending on the direction of the transaction.
10.5.4. Circle France keeps a dematerialised record of each FX Operation executed. Clients may access their full FX Operation history directly via their Circle Mint Account. Upon request, Circle France will provide a digital receipt for any FX Operation performed, indicating the nature of the transaction, the currencies involved, the amounts exchanged, and the exchange rate applied.
10.5.5. For the avoidance of doubt, the present exchange between EURC and USDC does not constitute an exchange of crypto-assets within the meaning of Article 3(20) of the MiCA Regulation. Each such exchange operation is carried out exclusively in the form of (i) the redemption of an e-money token, (ii) a fiat foreign exchange transaction under Circle France’s EMI license, and (iii) the issuance of a new e-money token.
