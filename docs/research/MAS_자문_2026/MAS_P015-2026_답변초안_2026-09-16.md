## 3. 답할 질문과 안 답할 질문

답한다: Q4(현금성 비율 임계값), Q23(주체별 준비자산 공개), Q29(은행 별도 법인과 모은행 예치 집중), Q30(공시 형식 21C와 검증 기한 37조에 대한 의견).

안 답한다: 정의, 인가, 이자 금지, 캡, 시스템적 지정, 외국 코인 인정. 데이터가 없고 프로젝트 범위 밖.

**Respondent:** Insung Kang, undergraduate student, Ajou University (Republic of Korea). Independent student research project "StableWatch" on reserve disclosure standards for won-denominated stablecoins. Not affiliated with any issuer, bank or regulator. [공개 여부: 이름과 제출물 공개에 동의 / 제출물만 공개하고 이름은 비공개 중 택일]

**Scope of this response.** I respond only to Questions 4, 23, 29 and 30, on reserve asset composition and public disclosure. My comments are based on a small empirical exercise: I parsed the four most recent monthly independent accountant examination reports published by Circle for USDC (reference dates 30 Jan, 31 Mar, 30 Apr and 31 Jul 2026) and measured what the largest fiat stablecoin issuer actually does. The sample is small (one issuer, one currency, four months) and I present it as evidence of feasibility, not as a market average.

**Question 4 — minimum proportion of reserve assets in cash and bank deposits (paras 3.6 to 3.7).**

MAS notes that the UK and EU require 5% to 30% for non-systemic stablecoins. In the four reports I examined, the share of USDC reserves held outside the government money market fund (that is, cash at banks plus, in one month, directly held Treasuries) was 13.82%, 13.95%, 14.57% and 15.56%, a mean of 14.47%. The remainder was held in a government money market fund invested in short-dated US Treasuries and overnight repo. Two observations follow.

1. A threshold of 30% would require the largest issuer in the market to roughly double its cash and deposit holdings. A threshold in the range of 10% to 15% is consistent with observed practice while still guaranteeing same-day liquidity for a meaningful share of redemptions.

2. Whatever threshold is chosen, the disclosure regime under section 21C should require the issuer to publish the cash and deposit share on every reference date, to four decimal places of the coverage ratio and with the asset categories defined in the notice, so that compliance with the threshold can be verified from the published report rather than only from supervisory returns. In the reports I examined, the cash share had to be reconstructed from a category labelled "other reserve assets", which in one month mixed cash with directly held Treasuries. A prescribed category list would remove that ambiguity.

**Question 23 — reserve assets held across multiple issuing entities (para 5.7).**

I support the proposal that the amount of reserve assets held by each issuing entity be reported monthly. I suggest that the same information be included in the public disclosure under section 21C, not only in submissions to MAS. The rationale MAS gives in para 5.7, that bifurcation of the reserve pool across entities creates a risk that one entity cannot meet redemptions, applies equally to any structure in which the reserve pool is split across several custodians or several affiliated entities within one jurisdiction. Holders and market participants can only price that risk if the split is public.

**Question 29 — banks issuing through a separate non-bank entity (paras 6.3 to 6.4).**

I support the separate-entity requirement. Para 6.3 explicitly contemplates that the reserve pool may consist of cash deposited with the parent bank. This creates a concentration in which the asset backing the stablecoin is a claim on the same banking group that controls the issuer. I suggest two disclosure items under section 21C for such issuers:

1. the name of each custodian or deposit-taking institution holding reserve assets, with the amount held at each, on every reference date; and

2. the share of reserve assets held with the parent bank or any related corporation, so that the degree of intra-group concentration is visible.

This is the structure under discussion in Korea, where the central bank has proposed that won stablecoins be issued by bank-led consortia. In that context the per-custodian breakdown is the only way to see whether reserves are concentrated in one member bank. I note that voluntary disclosure of the custodian bank by name already exists in practice: two Korean digital asset exchanges publish the name of the bank holding customer won deposits in their periodic attestation reports without any legal obligation to do so, which suggests the cost of this item is low.

**Question 30 — other comments: attestation timing under new section 37(1A) and (1C), and the reference date rule.**

Section 37(1C) requires publication of each reserve assets attestation report "within such time as the Authority may specify". When MAS sets that period, I would ask it to take into account the observed time needed for independent examination. In the four Circle reports, the interval from the reference date to the accountant's signature was 27, 29, 28 and 27 calendar days, or 19 to 21 business days. A publication deadline shorter than that for an independently examined report would not be met by the largest issuer in the market. A workable design, which I have adopted in my own draft standard, separates two documents: an issuer-prepared, management-signed monthly statement due within five business days of the reference date, and an independently examined report due within 30 days after the end of each quarter (or after each month, if monthly examination is required, with a deadline of not less than 25 business days).

On reference dates: one of the four reports used 30 January rather than the month-end because 31 January 2026 fell on a Saturday. A rule of "last calendar day of the month" alone cannot be followed in every month; I would suggest "last calendar day, or the preceding business day where the last day is not a business day", stated in the notice under section 21C.

**Methodological note.** I am building an open, rule-based checker that reads published reserve reports and tests them against the disclosure rules of six jurisdictions, including the MAS-SCS framework. If it would be useful to MAS, I would be glad to share the parsed data and the rule set. [공개 URL이 생기면 여기에]
